Installation Instructions:

STEP 1:
You can either use the provided resourcepack zip file provided for your server or you can combine it with your current resourcepack although it may prompt you that its going to overwrite the 'leather_horse_armor.json' that is standard procedure for the ModelEngine.

STEP 2:
Please make sure you have MagicCrates or AquaticCrates, ModelEngine and Oraxen installed.
Copy both 'MagicCrates'/'AquaticCrates', 'Oraxen' and 'ModelEngine' into your plugins folder of your server.

STEP 3:
Restart the server and check it out, you can take the chest with the MagicCrates commands '/magiccrates editor' and then choose chest which you need and in the interface take chest.
With AquaticCrates you can take it with commands:
/acrates crate give fairbg1 
/acrates crate give fairbg2
/acrates crate give fairbg3

And keys for this:
/acrates key give fairbg1
/acrates key give fairbg2
/acrates key give fairbg3


DISCLAIMER:
there are some drawbacks to using ModelEngine that may get patched out in the future (whoknows?) but sometimes the model parts
will separate, that's just the nature of how this plugins works at the moment.

You can remedy this by going into the ModelEngine config.yml and changing the parameter 'Force-Update-Tick:' and set it to
1 for a faster refreshing, although this has the potential lag a lower spec server.
